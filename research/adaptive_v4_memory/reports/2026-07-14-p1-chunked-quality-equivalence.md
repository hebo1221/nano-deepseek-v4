# P1 chunked quality-path equivalence

Date: 2026-07-14  
Decision: **chunk=2 accepted for P2 quality shards; larger/full chunks rejected**

The core P2 policy set contains native, fixed 1x/2x/4x, and calibrated
hierarchical 1x/2x/4x. To reduce the cost of long-generation quality evaluation,
causal decode was grouped into small chunks while retaining the same prefill,
cache, and controller state transitions.

Chunk size 2 was compared against the frozen token-by-token pilot raw data on
all nine families, five contexts, and 180 conversations. All 1,260
policy-conversation records and all 3,920 predictions were identical. The
summed chunked forward time was 380.84 seconds on the recorded reference host.

Full-sequence and larger chunk candidates produced at least one prediction
mismatch during validation and were rejected. They are not used to generate
quality claims. Chunk=2 is accepted only for the Tier-S quality matrix; it does
not represent the physical tier, transfer, latency, or fused-runtime path.
Those measurements remain separate P4 experiments.
